🎵 Music

Classical music was a big part of my life growing up, I've been playing violin since I was seven years old. I played most intensively in high school, and I spent a couple of summers in classical music training programs like the Boston University Tanglewood Institute and in the Advanced String Quartet program at Interlochen Arts Camp. I kept playing some throughout undergrad, where I was the associate concertmaster in the Corvallis-OSU Symphony. I also played in a wonderful chamber group and we spent a few years traveling around Oregon to perform at various venues!

I might not play as intensively these days but I still love chamber music — if you're in the area and interested in collaborating or playing together, feel free to reach out!
